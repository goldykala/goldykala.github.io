About VerdaSafe

Our story & mission

Founded in Ludhiana (2014), VerdaSafe arose from a singular vision: make world-class food safety accessible for every Indian food business. We blend global standards with local pragmatism.

Mission: Empower food entrepreneurs with practical systems, training, and digital tools that protect consumers and unlock export opportunities.
