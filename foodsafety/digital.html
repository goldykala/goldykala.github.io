Digital Platforms

Smart Digital Solutions for Food Safety

Move from manual paperwork to practical digital platforms that make food safety management easier, faster, and more reliable.

Home / Digital Platforms

Food safety records should be easy to manage, easy to verify, and ready whenever they are needed. Many food businesses still depend on paper checklists, manual registers, Excel sheets, and WhatsApp follow-ups. This often leads to missed records, delayed reporting, poor traceability, and difficulty during audits.

At CQBS, we help food manufacturers, food handlers, kitchens, catering units, warehouses, restaurants, and food service businesses move from manual recordkeeping to simple and practical digital platforms. Our tools help teams monitor daily operations, maintain compliance records, track corrective actions, and generate reports with better accuracy and control.

Our digital solutions are designed around real food business operations. The focus is not just on software, but on making food safety management easier, faster, and more reliable.

Why Digital Food Safety Platforms Matter

In food safety, missing or incomplete records can create serious problems. A missed cleaning checklist, an incomplete temperature log, an unrecorded supplier detail, or a delayed corrective action can lead to audit findings, customer complaints, and compliance risks.

Digital platforms help food businesses:

  • Maintain real-time food safety records
  • Reduce dependency on paper-based registers
  • Improve traceability across production and supply chain activities
  • Track hygiene, cleaning, opening, and closing checks
  • Record corrective actions with responsibility and timelines
  • Prepare better for customer, regulatory, and certification audits
  • Provide senior management with instant, one-click access to reports on the current status of the business, eliminating the need to search multiple sources
  • Improve management visibility through dashboards and reports
  • Reduce communication gaps between workers, supervisors, and management

CQBS helps food businesses create digital systems that are simple for teams to use and useful for management review.

Our Solutions

Digital Platform Solutions

Purpose-built tools designed to digitize, streamline, and secure your food safety documentation and daily operations.

Traceability & Supply Chain Portals

Traceability is one of the most important parts of food safety management. A food business must know where raw materials came from, how they were handled, where they were used, and where the final product was supplied. CQBS develops traceability and supply chain portals that help food businesses record supplier details, batch information, material movement, production links, dispatch records, and supporting documents.

    Benefits for Your Food Business
  • Quickly track raw materials, finished products, suppliers, and batches
  • Support faster investigation during complaints, recalls, audit checks, and customer queries

Daily Digital Checklists

Daily checks are important for hygiene, cleaning, temperature control, pest control, equipment condition, personal hygiene, and food handling practices. When these checks are handled manually, records may be missed, delayed, or difficult to verify. CQBS creates digital checklists for food handlers, supervisors, quality teams, and operations staff. These checklists can be customized according to the department, process, and daily control requirements.

    Benefits for Your Food Business
  • Complete daily checks in a structured way
  • Management can review completion status, identify gaps, and take timely action before small issues become bigger problems

Opening & Closing Checks

Opening and closing checks are essential for restaurants, kitchens, catering units, bakeries, retail food outlets, and food manufacturing units. These checks confirm that the workplace is clean, equipment is ready, temperatures are controlled, and safety requirements are followed before and after operations. CQBS helps create simple digital opening and closing check formats that are easy to fill, monitor, and review.

    Benefits for Your Food Business
  • Supervisors can confirm readiness before operations begin and ensure proper closure at the end of the day
  • Reduces hygiene risks, missed tasks, and operational confusion

Digital Audit Logbooks

Audit logbooks help businesses maintain records of inspections, internal audits, customer visits, hygiene rounds, non-conformities, observations, and follow-up actions. Paper-based logbooks are often difficult to track, search, and review. CQBS develops digital audit logbooks that allow teams to record audit observations, assign responsibility, attach evidence, and monitor closure status.

    Benefits for Your Food Business
  • Maintain a clear audit history and present evidence more confidently during inspections
  • Improves accountability, transparency, and audit readiness

Corrective Action Tracking

Corrective actions are effective only when they are properly assigned, followed, verified, and closed. In many food businesses, issues are recorded but not tracked until completion. CQBS builds corrective action tracking systems where issues can be recorded, assigned to responsible persons, given deadlines, updated with evidence, and reviewed by management.

    Benefits for Your Food Business
  • Close non-conformities on time and reduce repeated issues
  • Supports stronger compliance, better follow-up, and continuous improvement

Custom Websites for Food Businesses

A professional website helps food businesses present their services, certifications, products, facilities, enquiry options, and business credibility. It is especially useful for food manufacturers, food service companies, consultants, laboratories, training providers, and food brands. CQBS supports food businesses with custom website development based on their services, target customers, and compliance needs.

    Benefits for Your Food Business
  • Professional online presence that builds trust
  • Improves customer communication and supports enquiry generation

Cloud-Based Dashboard & Reports

Management needs clear and updated information to make better decisions. When records are stored in different registers, files, or spreadsheets, it becomes difficult to understand pending actions, recurring issues, checklist completion, and overall compliance status. CQBS helps create cloud-based dashboards and reports that show key food safety activities, checklist status, audit findings, corrective actions, supplier status, and operational performance.

    Benefits for Your Food Business
  • Management team can review important information from one place
  • Saves time, improves visibility, and supports faster decision-making
